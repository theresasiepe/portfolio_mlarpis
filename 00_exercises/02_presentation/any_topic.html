The Fisher- Yates Shuffle Technique

\[ \begin{equation} \begin{aligned} &\text{Input: } A \text{ is a sequence of } n \text{ symbols} \\[2mm] &\textbf{For } i = n-1 \text{ down to } 1 \textbf{ Do} \\ &\hspace{1cm} T[i] = \text{random number } j \in [0,i] \\ &\textbf{End For} \\[2mm] &\textbf{For } i = n-1 \text{ down to } 1 \textbf{ Do} \\ &\hspace{1cm} j = T[i] \\ &\hspace{1cm} \textbf{If } j \neq i \textbf{ Then} \\ &\hspace{2cm} B = A[j] \\ &\hspace{2cm} A[j] = A[i] \\ &\hspace{2cm} A[i] = B \\ &\hspace{1cm} \textbf{End If} \\ &\textbf{End For} \end{aligned} \end{equation} \]

Motivation Music

I do not only like to listen to music; I also love to do sports. Listening to music makes my runs easier. This subjective feeling is supported by meta-analytical results which show that listening to music while exercising enhances enjoyment and reduces the perceived exertion (Terry et al. 2020).

References

Terry, Peter C., Costas I. Karageorghis, Michelle L. Curran, Olwenn V. Martin, and Renée L. Parsons-Smith. 2020. “Effects of Music in Exercise and Sport: A Meta-Analytic Review.” Psychological Bulletin 146 (2): 91–117. https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000216.
